## open-dict-data/ipa-dict (English US data)

The optional General American IPA lookup uses the `data/en_US.txt` file from
`open-dict-data/ipa-dict`, pinned to commit
`43c3570eb3553bdd19fccd2bd0091534889af023`. The project credits the English US
data to a modified `cmudict-ipa` dataset with stress markers from `syllabify`.
The application converts that source notation to broad learner IPA before
displaying it; in particular, stressed STRUT is written `/ʌ/` and `/ɫ/` is
broadened to `/l/`. Rhotic vowels and `/ɹ/` are also converted to conventional
learner notation.

Source: https://github.com/open-dict-data/ipa-dict

## JoseLlarena/Britfone (English UK data)

The optional British IPA lookup uses `britfone.main.3.0.1.csv` from
`JoseLlarena/Britfone`, pinned to commit
`1062be14adc96c358f2087ac5449d72130c7a6f4`. The application converts the
source's strict IPA to familiar broad learner notation and moves stress marks
from the vowel to the start of the stressed syllable.

Source: https://github.com/JoseLlarena/Britfone
